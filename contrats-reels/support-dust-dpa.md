This Data Processing Addendum ("DPA") forms part of, and is subject to, the Dust Terms & Conditions (or Master Services Agreement in case of an Enterprise plan) between Dust ("Dust") and Customer that reference this DPA (the “Agreement”), and is effective on the Effective Date of the Agreement.

This DPA applies where, and to the extent that, Dust processes Customer Personal Data as Processor, on behalf of Customer acting as Controller, when providing Services under the Agreement. All capitalized terms not defined in this DPA shall have the meanings set forth in the Agreement.

Without prejudice to the foregoing, Dust and the Customer may have access to personal data of natural persons acting as contact points, provided by the other party including employees, representatives or agents of the latter, which it may process as Controller in the context of the proper performance of this Agreement and compliance with their legal and regulatory obligations which are imposed on it. It is the responsibility of each party to inform the data subjects, whose personal data it has disclosed, of the processing carried out by the other party and of the provisions of this DPA.

## 1\. Definitions

| Term | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Data Breach | means any unauthorized or unlawful breach of security that leads to the accidental or unlawful destruction, loss, alteration, unauthorized disclosure of or access to Customer Personal Data. |
| Data Protection Laws | means all data protection and privacy laws applicable to the processing of Personal Data under the Agreement, including, where applicable, EU Data Protection Laws and UK Data Protection Laws. |
| Data Controller or Controller | means an entity that determines the purposes and means of the processing of Personal Data. |
| Data Processor or Processor | means an entity that processes Personal Data under the instructions and on behalf of a Data Controller. |
| EEA | means the European Economic Area. |
| EU Data Protection Laws | means Regulation 2016/679 of the European Parliament and of the Council on the protection of natural persons with regard to the processing of Personal Data and on the free movement of such data (General Data Protection Regulation) ("GDPR"). |
| EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework | means the EU-U.S. EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework approved by the European Commission pursuant to Decision C(2023) 4745 of 10 July 2023. |
| Personal Data | means any information relating to an identified or identifiable natural person. |
| Processing | has the meaning given to it in the GDPR and "process", "processes" and "processed" will be interpreted accordingly. |
| Sub-Processor | means any Data Processor engaged by Dust or its Affiliates to assist in fulfilling its obligations with respect to providing the Services pursuant to the Agreement or this DPA. Sub-Processors may include third parties or Dust’s Affiliates. |
| UK Data Protection Law | means the UK Data Protection Act 2018 as amended by the Data Protection, Privacy and Electronic Communications (Amendments etc) (EU Exit) Regulations 2019, or any other law or case law in the United Kingdom which is applicable to the Processing. |

## 2\. Relationship with the Agreement

2.1 The parties agree that this DPA shall replace any existing DPA or other data protection provisions the parties may have previously entered into in connection with the Services.

## 3\. Scope of this DPA

3.1 This DPA applies where and only to the extent that Dust processes Personal Data that originates from the EEA and the UK and/or that is otherwise subject to EU Data Protection Laws or UK Data Protection Laws on behalf of Customer in the course of providing Services to the Customer pursuant to the Agreement.

## 4\. Parties’ qualification

4.1 As part of the provision of the Services provided under this Agreement, Dust is processing the Customer Personal Data under the instructions and on behalf of the Customer.

4.2 Customer is the Data Controller of Customer Personal Data and Dust is  the Data Processor acting on behalf of Customer.

## 5\. Customer’s obligations

5.1 Customer agrees that (i) it will comply with its obligations as a Data Controller under Data Protection Laws in respect of its processing of Customer Personal Data and any processing instructions it issues to Dust; and (ii) it ensures that the processing is lawful so that Dust can process Customer Personal Data pursuant to the Agreement and this DPA and in compliance with Data Protection Laws.

5.2 It is the Customer's responsibility to provide information (in accordance with the requirements of the Data Protection Laws) to data subjects at the time of collection of their Personal Data.

5.3 Customer agrees not to provide Dust with any special category of data as defined under Article 9 of the GDPR.

## 6\. Dust’s obligations

6.1 Dust will process Customer Personal Data in accordance with Customer’s documented instructions. Customer agrees that this DPA, the Agreement and any subsequent statements of work or services orders (agreed in writing between the Parties), and any Services’ configurations made by Customer or its authorized users (in accordance with the Documentation), comprise Customer’s complete instructions to Dust regarding the Processing of Customer Personal Data. Any additional or alternate instructions must be agreed between the parties in writing, including the costs (if any) associated with complying with such instructions.

6.2 Dust is not responsible for determining if Customer’s instructions are compliant with applicable law. However, if Dust is of the opinion that a Customer instruction infringes applicable Data Protection Laws, Dust shall notify Customer as soon as reasonably practicable and shall not be required to comply with such infringing instruction.

6.3 Details of Data Processing

Details of Data Processing - which refer to the Customer Personal Data uploaded to the Services by the Customer - are set forth in Exhibit B.

Customer will provide Dust in writing with reasonable prior notice if it intends to make any changes to the aforementioned Data Processing, including but not limited to Customer Personal Data processed. If needed, Exhibit B will be amended accordingly.

6.4 Register of categories of personal data processing activities

Dust undertakes to keep a register of all categories of Customer Personal Data Processing activities carried out on behalf of Customer in accordance with the provisions of Article 30 of the GDPR. Dust shall provide Customer with its related information as contained in the relevant register upon request.

## 7\. Sub-processing

7.1 Authorized Sub-Processors. Customer agrees that in order to provide the Services, Dust may engage Sub-Processors to process Customer Personal Data. Dust maintains a list of its authorized Sub-Processors on its website at [![](https://dust-tt.notion.site/image/https%3A%2F%2Fprod-files-secure.s3.us-west-2.amazonaws.com%2Fa7cb39c2-7827-440b-828e-608471287d89%2Fd322f29c-d50a-4593-aebc-d7e178bc0540%2FAppIcon_228.png?table=block&id=cb656ff3-093c-4aee-9e08-0e3021e71f9c&spaceId=a7cb39c2-7827-440b-828e-608471287d89&width=200&userId=&cache=v2&imgBuildSrc=renderMentionIcon)Sub-Processors](https://dust-tt.notion.site/p/Sub-Processors-cb656ff3093c4aee9e080e3021e71f9c?pvs=24) and Customer authorizes Dust to rely on these Sub-Processors. The list of Sub-Processors applicable as of the Effective Date of the Agreement is attached hereto as Exhibit D of this DPA.

7.2 Sub-Processor Obligations. Where Dust is authorized to rely on Sub-Processors as described in Section 6.1:

(a) Dust will enter into a written agreement with the Sub-Processor imposing data protection terms substantially similar to those contained in this DPA and that require the Sub-Processor to protect the Customer Personal Data to the standard required by Data Protection Laws; and

(b) Dust will remain responsible for the compliance of the Sub-Processor with the obligations of this DPA.

7.3 New Sub-Processors. Dust will provide Customer with ten (10) days prior notice if it intends to make any changes to its Sub-Processors list. Customer may object in writing during this period to Dust’s appointment of a new Sub-Processor (or a material change affecting an existing Sub-Processor), provided that such objection is based on reasonable grounds relating to data protection. Such objection may be sent by email to the Dust’s DPO (“Data Protection Officer”), contact specified in Exhibit A, with the following information: Customer, Sub-Processor, Grounds for Objection, Requestor Name, Requestor Email. In such event, the parties will discuss such concerns in good faith with a view to achieving resolution. If this is not possible, Customer may suspend or terminate the Agreement (without prejudice to any fees incurred by Customer prior to suspension or termination).

## 8\. Security Measures and Data Breach Response

8.1 Security Measures. Dust has implemented and will maintain appropriate technical and organizational security measures to protect Customer Personal Data from Security Incidents and to preserve the security and confidentiality of the Customer Personal Data ("Security Measures"). The latest Security Measures applicable to the Services are available [online](https://dust-tt.notion.site/17bb854ffc674e1ba729d1a10837e50d?v=de92d1770a344beeafe9f701e78ad8f3).

8.2 Updates to Security Measures. Customer is responsible for reviewing the information made available by Dust relating to data security and for making an independent determination as to whether the Services meet Customer’s requirements and legal obligations under Data Protection Laws. Customer acknowledges that the Security Measures are subject to technical progress and evolution and that Dust may update or modify the Security Measures from time to time provided that such updates and modifications maintain a similar or higher level of protection of the overall security of the Services purchased by the Customer.

8.3 Personnel. Dust restricts its personnel from processing Customer Personal Data without authorization by Dust as set forth in the Security Measures, and shall ensure that any person who is authorized by Dust to process Customer Personal Data is under an appropriate obligation of confidentiality.

8.4 Customer Responsibilities. Notwithstanding the above, Customer agrees that except as provided by this DPA, Customer is responsible for its secure use of the Services. Customer may elect to implement technical or organization measures in relation to Customer Data, which may include (i) protecting account authentication credentials; (ii) protecting the security of Customer Data when in transit to and from the Services; (iii) implementing measures to backup and archive appropriately Customer Data, using their own methods, in order to restore availability and access to Customer Data in a timely manner in the event of a physical or technical incident; and (iv) taking any appropriate steps to securely encrypt, anonymize or pseudonymize any Customer Personal Data uploaded to the Dust’s Services.

8.5 Data Breach Response. Upon becoming aware of a Data Breach that affects Customer Personal Data, Dust will notify Customer without undue delay after becoming aware of the Data Breach  and will provide information to the Customer relating to the Data Breach in line with Article 33.2 of the GDPR. Dust will also take reasonable steps to mitigate and, where possible, to remedy the effects of any Data Breach.

## 9\. Audits

9.1 Audits. Dust will, upon reasonable notice (not less than thirty (30) days) and not more than once a year (unless there is a material Data Breach), allow its procedures and documentation to be inspected or audited by Customer (or its designee, excluding any Dust competitor) during business hours, in order to ascertain compliance with the obligations set forth in this DPA. For the avoidance of doubt, the scope of such audit shall be limited to documents and records allowing the verification of Dust’s compliance with the obligations set forth in this DPA and shall not include any financial documents or Dust’s records, or any documents or records or Personal Data concerning other customers of Dust. Any such audits will be conducted at Customer’s expense.

## 10\. International Transfers

10.1  Subject to the following, Dust may transfer and process Customer Personal Data to its Affiliates or its Sub-Processors that maintain data processing operations and that can be located outside the EEA or the UK. Dust will at all times provide an adequate level of protection for the Customer Personal Data processed, in accordance with the requirements of Data Protection Laws, by relying on the Standard Contractual Clauses. Where relevant, Dust has indeed entered into agreements with its Sub-Processors that incorporate the Standard Contractual Clauses as approved by the European Commission in Decision (EU) 2021/914, Module three (processor to processor) or EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as appropriate, and/or where relevant, the UK Addendum.

10.2  Customer hereby consents to processing of the Customer Personal Data in the countries set out in Exhibit D (List of Sub-Processors), as necessary to help ensure the proper functioning of and improvement to the services.

10.3  When the Customer is located outside the EEA or the UK, Customer and Dust are entering into Module four (processor to controller), and/or where relevant, the UK Addendum, or relying on the EU-U.S. Data Privacy Framework, as appropriate.

## 11\. Return or Deletion of Data

11.1 Upon termination or expiration of the Agreement, Dust shall (at Customer's election) delete or return to Customer all Customer Personal Data in its possession or control in accordance with the terms of the Agreement. This requirement shall not apply to the extent Dust is required by applicable law to retain some or all of the Customer Personal Data. Moreover, this requirement shall also not apply to Customer Personal Data that Dust has archived on back-up systems or that might be stored in Usage Data, provided that Dust shall securely isolate and protect such Customer Personal Data from any further processing, except to the extent required by law.

## 12\. Cooperation

12.1 To the extent that Customer is unable to independently access the relevant Customer Personal Data within the Services and provided that Customer has configured the Services in accordance with Dust’s recommendations, Dust shall (at Customer's expense) provide reasonable cooperation to assist Customer to respond to any requests from individuals or applicable data protection authorities relating to the processing of Customer Personal Data under the Agreement. In the event that any such request is made directly to Dust, Dust will forward the request to Customer and will not respond to such communication.  If Dust is legally required to respond to such a request, Dust will promptly notify Customer and coordinate with Customer to provide an appropriate answer.

12.2 If a law enforcement agency sends Dust a demand for Customer Personal Data (for example, through a subpoena or court order), Dust will attempt to redirect the law enforcement agency to request that data directly from Customer. As part of this effort, Dust may provide Customer’s basic contact information to the law enforcement agency and will notify Customer. If compelled to disclose Customer Personal Data to a law enforcement agency, then Dust will give Customer reasonable notice of the demand to allow Customer to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy unless Dust is legally prohibited from doing so.

12.3 To the extent Dust is required under EU Data Protection Laws, Dust will (at Customer's expense) provide reasonably requested information regarding the Services to enable the Customer to carry out data protection impact assessments and prior consultations with data protection authorities as required by law.

## 13\. Liability

13.1 For the avoidance of doubt, any claim or remedies the Customer may have against Dust, any of its Affiliates and their respective employees, agents and Sub-Processors arising under or in connection with this DPA, including: (i) for breach of this DPA; (ii) as a result of fines (administrative, regulatory or otherwise) imposed upon Customer; and (iii) under EU Data Protection Laws and/or UK Data Protection Laws where applicable, including any claims relating to damages paid to a data subject, will be subject to any limitation of liability provisions (including any agreed aggregate financial cap) that apply under the Agreement. Customer further agrees that any regulatory penalties incurred by Dust in relation to the Customer Personal Data that arise as a result of, or in connection with, Customer’s failure to comply with its obligations under this DPA or any applicable Data Protection Laws shall count toward and reduce Dust’s liability under the Agreement as if it were liability to the Customer under the Agreement. Customer shall defend, indemnify and hold harmless Dust and Dust’s directors, officers, employees, representatives, and agents from and against any and all claims, actions, demands, and legal proceedings and all liabilities, damages, losses, judgments, authorized settlements, costs, fines, penalties and expenses including reasonable attorneys’ fees arising out of or in connection with violation by the Customer of any Data Protection Laws.

13.2 Any claims against Dust or its Affiliates under this DPA shall be brought solely against the entity that is a party to the Agreement.

13.3 In no event shall any party limit its liability with respect to any individual's data protection rights under this DPA or otherwise.

## 14\. Governing law

14.1 This DPA will be governed by and construed in accordance with governing law and jurisdiction provisions in the Agreement, unless required otherwise by applicable Data Protection Laws.

## 15\. Severability

15.1 The provisions of this DPA are severable. If any phrase, clause or provision is invalid or unenforceable in whole or in part, such invalidity or unenforceability shall affect only such phrase, clause or provision, and the rest of this DPA shall remain in full force and effect.

## 16\. Miscellaneous

16.1 No one other than a party to this DPA, their successors and permitted assignees shall have any right to enforce any of its terms.

16.2 Except for the changes made by this DPA, the Agreement remains unchanged and in full force and effect. If there is any conflict between this DPA and the Agreement, this DPA shall prevail to the extent of that conflict.

### Exhibit B: Data Processing Details

This exhibit details the data processing where Dust (“Data Processor”) processes Customer Personal Data on behalf of Customer (“Controller”) to provide Services under the Agreement.

A. List of Parties

Data Importer. Data importer is Customer.

Data Exporter. Data exporter is Dust (Data Processor).

B. Data Processing Details

Data subjects. Given the nature of Services, Customer will be expected to transfer Personal Data for the following categories: Customer’s employees, clients, end users,contractors and suppliers.

Customer Personal Data Categories. Last name, first name, pseudonym, contact information details including e-mail, telephone number, company, work location, professional occupation, position within an organization, userID (User's technical identifier), User IP address, account information, unstructured information contained in documents or user requests.

Special categories. None, i.e. no genetic, biometric, health data, no data revealing racial and ethnic origin, political opinions, religious or ideological convictions, trade union affiliation, or sexual orientation, criminal convictions or offenses.

Transfer Frequency. Customer Personal Data will be transferred on a continuous basis between Customer and Dust when Customer is located outside the EU.

Nature and Purpose. The nature and purpose of the processing of Customer Personal Data is to provide Dust’s Services as described in the Agreement.

Duration. The duration of the Customer Personal Data processing corresponds to the term of the Agreement.

Processing Operations. The personal data transferred will be subject to the following basic processing activities: collection, consultation, semi-automatic and/or automatic processing, extraction, conservation, storage, analysis, deletion, hosting.

C. Customer Instructions

Some Customer may choose to enable specific features. Depending on the Customer’s configurations and instructions as presented in Dust Documentation, such features can enable the processing of Customer Data by Dust on behalf of the Customer. In such case, Customer, as data controller, is responsible for determining the appropriate legal basis for such processing, providing required notices to its users, and obtaining any authorization required by applicable law. Dust continues to act as data processor under the Customer’s specific instructions. The Customer acknowledges and recognizes that the activation of a feature is considered as documented instructions of the Customer to Dust, in line with this, this Data Processing Addendum. Information about the data processing of a dedicated feature is accessible in the Dust Documentation.
